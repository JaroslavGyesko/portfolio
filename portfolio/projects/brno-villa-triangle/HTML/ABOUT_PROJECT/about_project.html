    About project

    The project "Brno Villa Triangle" creates a unique space combining cultural, historical and architectural elements of three exceptional buildings ‑ Villas Tugendhat, Arnold and Löw Beer. This triangle offers a wide range of experiences for diverse groups of visitors. This ambitious idea brings a unique triangle of cultural heritage.


    The aim of this project is not only to raise awareness of these exceptional spaces, but also to offer a diverse program for a wide range of visitors. Families with children can enjoy interactive programs and educational games, while architecture lovers from other countries can look forward to expertly guided tours in different languages, highlighting the architectural features and history of individual buildings. A single entrance fee for all three villas brings flexibility and simplification for visitors, who can take advantage of discounts when visiting several villas at the same time. This creates a synergy between these cultural treasures and supports tourism in Brno.


    The digital connection of the villas through a new visual identity and website promotes a modern approach and facilitates the purchase of tickets. Cooperation with contribution organizations such as the Museum of Brno and the Museum of the City of Brno ensures efficient management of reservations and overall organization of visits. Revitalizing the area of the "Brno Villa Triangle" through educational trails, benches, gazebos and a herb garden increases the attractiveness for different groups of visitors. This makes the project not only a place of culture, but also of relaxation and connection with nature.


    A fundamental part of the project is the social connection of the villas. Unique events, such as Hanukkah and Purim celebrations, connect history and the present, offering visitors an unforgettable cultural experience. Cooperation with Brno businesses and the organization of exhibitions and concerts within the "Triangle of Famous Villas and Gardens" contributes to the enrichment of cultural life in Brno. Connecting the villa with businesses and creating a joint souvenir, for example a board game, gives visitors the opportunity to take home not only the experience, but also a reminder of their knowledge of the unique history and architecture of the Triangle of Famous Villas and Gardens. Overall, this project not only offers a rich cultural experience for visitors, but also contributes to enriching the local cultural heritage. With its comprehensive approach and ambition to involve different groups of visitors, it could become an exceptional site and a proud representative on the UNESCO list.
